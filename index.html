QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

THE DETAILS MATTER.

How do I know which pest I have?

Appearance, droppings, damage patterns, nesting sites, and activity can provide clues. Professional identification may be necessary before choosing a control method.

Are all pest problems treated the same way?

No. Different species and property conditions require different approaches. Correct identification helps avoid unnecessary or ineffective treatments.

Can I prevent pests without pesticides?

Many preventive measures do not involve pesticides. Sealing entry points, reducing moisture, maintaining sanitation, and removing food sources may help.

Are pest control products safe for pets and children?

Safety depends on the specific product, application, exposure, and label instructions. Discuss children, pets, and other concerns before any treatment. All pesticide applications must follow applicable laws and product labeling.
